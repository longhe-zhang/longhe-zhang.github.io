About

Media, memory, news, and emerging technologies.

I am a PhD student in Internet and New Media at Sichuan University. My research examines how media technologies reshape information, memory, trust, and everyday social life, with current interests in digital journalism, media sociology, collective memory, and generative AI and news.

My work moves between qualitative inquiry and comparative or computational approaches. Across different projects, I am interested in the infrastructures, interfaces, and practices through which people encounter news, preserve memories, negotiate technological constraints, and evaluate emerging forms of mediated information.
