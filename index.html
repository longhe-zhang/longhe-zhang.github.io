About

Longhe Zhang is a PhD student in Internet and New Media at Sichuan University. His work sits at the intersection of journalism studies, media sociology, memory studies, and emerging media technologies.

He is especially interested in how digital infrastructures and interfaces reorganize practices of remembering, news use, trust, and social interaction. His research combines qualitative inquiry with comparative and computational approaches.
